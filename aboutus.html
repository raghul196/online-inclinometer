About Online Inclinometer

Hi, I'm Raghul Babu, a software developer, and I built this inclinometer.

It started with a roof-pitch problem while I was planning solar panels. Every solar calculator, including PV Freund, the planning tool I build, asks for the roof pitch first. Phones already have a sensor that knows which way is down, so I wrote a small web page to read it. I soon realised that everyone who makes things, from carpenters to DIYers, needs a quick angle finder, so I turned it into this free tool. My aim is to keep it simple enough that you can skip the how-to and get straight to the job.

What the tool does

  • Shows pitch (front to back) and roll (side to side) in real time, in whole degrees.
  • Shows a bubble that turns green when the surface is level.
  • Has a Zero button that sets any surface as 0°, so you can measure one surface against another or cancel the tilt from a camera bump.
  • Can beep when it's level (turn on Audio Feedback in Settings), so you can level something without watching the screen.
  • Shows a QR code on computers, which usually lack motion sensors, so you can open it on your phone.
  • Is available in 12 languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Indonesian and Russian.

I also write guides on the jobs I've used it for, like measuring a roof pitch, levelling a washing machine and building a ramp.

Your data

The inclinometer runs entirely in your browser. Sensor readings are processed on your phone and are never sent anywhere. There's no account and nothing to install. I count page views with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies, and the site may display ads served by Google AdSense. The privacy policy has the details.

Accuracy

This uses your phone's motion sensors, so it is not a certified instrument. It works well for DIY and everyday jobs. For better results, take a reading, turn the phone 180° on the same spot, and average the two. Don't rely on it for structural, safety-critical or professional work. See the terms of use.
