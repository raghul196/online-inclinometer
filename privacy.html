Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

This policy explains what data online-inclinometer.com handles, covering the inclinometer tool and the guides. The site is run by Raghul Babu ("I" or "me").

1. Sensor data stays on your device

The inclinometer reads your device's motion sensors through your browser to calculate pitch and roll. All of this happens on your device, in real time.

  • Sensor readings and measurements are never sent to me or anyone else.
  • Nothing is saved, not even on your device. When you close the page, the readings are gone.
  • There are no accounts, and I don't ask for your name, email or any other personal information to use the tool.

2. Device permissions

Some browsers, such as Safari on iPhone, ask for permission to access motion and orientation before the tool can work. This permission is used only to read the sensors for the angle calculation. You can refuse it, but then the inclinometer can't show readings.

3. Analytics (GoatCounter)

The tool pages use GoatCounter to count page views, so I know roughly how many people use the site and which language versions they open. GoatCounter doesn't use cookies and doesn't store your IP address. It records the page visited, the referring page, your browser and operating system, your screen size and an approximate country. I can't use this to identify you.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. If it does:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • Google explains how it uses data from sites that show its ads at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

I don't sell your data, and I don't share anything with advertisers myself. Ad cookies are set and controlled by Google, not by me.

5. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when pages are requested, for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Cookies and local storage

This site doesn't set any cookies of its own and doesn't use your browser's local storage. The only cookies that may be set are Google's advertising cookies, described above. You can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings.

7. Children

The site isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

8. Changes to this policy

If I change how the site handles data, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
